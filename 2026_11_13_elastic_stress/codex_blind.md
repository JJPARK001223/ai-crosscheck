# Codex blind source audit
Primary source independently opened: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jmmp/3/10/3_10_1115/_pdf (2009, pp1116,1118,1119,1123).
Eq5 supplies K=f1*sigma*sqrt(pi*ae), but surrounding prose explains f1 and ae, not the stress convention. Section3.2 says f1 was computed using elastic FEA and Eq5, without explicit M-to-sigma formula. Local full-text searches found no nominal/bending stress definition. Thus none of outer MRo/I, inner MRi/I, thin-wall M/(pi Rm^2 t), or sigma_y M/ML is confirmed by explicit definition.
Eq6 rewritten gives ae/a=1+[(n-1)/(6(n+1))]*f1^2*(sigma/sigma_y)^2/(1+q^2). Eq9 with omega=(n-1)/(6(n+1)), lambda1 eta2 replaces that stress ratio by q=M/ML. Exact algebraic equivalence would require sigma/sigma_y=q; the author says almost identical, so it cannot by itself establish a stress definition. This is a real ambiguity, not a license to use reference stress as confirmed.
The 2009 INSS Japanese plastic-prestrain paper is a separate semi-elliptical case and does not establish this table's convention. The 2006 undulated-front paper is cited for numerical accuracy, not a direct definition for pipe bending.
Current Excel independently calculated geometric I and outer nominal sigma: Rm600, Ri570, Ro630mm, I40816828392.50003 mm4, full M39922800000 Nmm, sigma_outer616.20084143MPa. These are uncracked-section mechanics quantities only; matching low-load FE must not determine f1 convention. Do not connect Table1 f1 directly to sigma_outer as an established paper reproduction.
